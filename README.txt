Bottlenecks section — responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript

Files:
- index.html: page markup
- styles.css: responsive layout and styling
- script.js: six cards, SVG icons, and reveal animation

How to run:
1. Keep all three files in the same folder.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. The sample photos load from Unsplash, so an internet connection is needed for those images.

Notes:
- Replace image URLs in script.js with your own garment-manufacturing photos for the closest match to the reference.
- The section is responsive: 3 columns on wide screens, 2 on medium screens, and 1 on mobile.
